Custode can lock the vault by itself when you stop using it, and it empties the clipboard shortly after you copy something. Here is how to set the lock and what to expect.

All platforms2 minutes

Set the automatic lock

  1. Open Settings

    With the vault unlocked. The setting is in the Database group and is saved for that vault.

  2. Choose Lock on inactivity

    Pick how long to wait: After 10 seconds, After 20 seconds, After 30 seconds, After 1 minute, After 2 minutes or After 5 minutes. With Never the vault does not lock while you have the app in front of you, and Settings shows the note Auto-lock is off.

What happens when it locks

When the time passes without you using the app, a dark screen covers the vault and shows for how long it has been locked. The vault is still open in memory: confirm it is you and you continue where you left off.

Unlock with biometrics
Fingerprint or face, when the device has them. Custode asks for it straight away.
Use master password
Appears when biometrics are not available or do not succeed. It takes you to the unlock screen, where you enter the master password.
Close the database instead
Closes the vault and goes back to the list of vaults, after asking you to confirm. The credentials saved for that vault on this device are removed.

The same time applies when Custode is in the background: if you come back after longer than that, you find it locked. With Never, a vault left in the background still locks after 15 minutes. While the app is in the background its content is covered by the Custode logo.

Lock straight away

In Settings, next to the Database title, the padlock button Lock vault locks the vault at once and takes you to the unlock screen.

The clipboard

When you copy a password, a one-time code or another field from Custode, the app empties the clipboard about 30 seconds later. It does so only if the clipboard still holds what you copied: if in the meantime you copied something else, it leaves it alone.

The 30 seconds are fixed. There is no setting to change them or to turn the clearing off. The clearing is done by the app itself, so it needs Custode still running when the time is up.
