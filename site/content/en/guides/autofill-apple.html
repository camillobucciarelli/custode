Custode can be one of the apps your iPhone, iPad or Mac uses to fill passwords. You pick the entry from a Custode list, in Safari and in other apps.

iPhone, iPad and Mac5 minutes

Before you start

You need a vault with at least one entry, unlocked in Custode. Custode prepares the entries for AutoFill when you unlock the vault and removes them when you lock it, so a locked vault offers nothing.

Turn it on, on iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Autofill & browsers

    In Custode open Settings and choose Autofill & browsers. While AutoFill is off, the card at the top reads Not enabled yet and lists the three steps to follow.

  2. Go to the system settings

    Tap Open iOS settings, or go there yourself: Settings → General → AutoFill & Passwords.

  3. Turn on AutoFill and pick Custode

    Follow the two steps the card shows: Turn on AutoFill Passwords, then Pick Custode in the list.

  4. Return to Custode

    The card now reads Autofill is on, and the row in Settings shows Autofill enabled.

Turn it on, on Mac

Custode for Mac includes the same password provider. Enable Custode in the system settings for passwords and autofill, then open Custode and unlock the vault. On a Mac, Autofill & browsers in Custode opens the setup for Chrome and Edge instead: see Autofill in Chrome and Edge.

Fill a login

  1. Select the username or password field

    In Safari or in an app, select the field and choose Custode among the password suggestions the system offers.

  2. Pick the entry

    The list Credentials opens with the entries that match that app or site; the closest one is marked Best match. Choose one and the username and password are filled. Custode never fills a field without you choosing the entry first.

  3. Search if nothing matches

    If you see No match for this app or site, use the field Search title, username, site, app to find the entry and choose it.

  4. Confirm the link in Custode

    After you fill from a search, the next time you open the vault Custode asks Link AutoFill credential? and shows the target, the entry and the username. Choose Link to remember that app or site for the entry, or Reject. The vault changes only after you confirm.

What is shared with AutoFill. Titles, usernames and sites of your entries, plus an encrypted copy of each password. All of it stays on the device.

If it does not work

  • The list shows No matching credentials: open Custode, unlock the vault and try again.
  • A search shows No credentials found: try another title, username, site or app.
  • Custode is not offered at all on iPhone or iPad: open Autofill & browsers in Custode. If the card reads Not enabled yet, repeat the steps above.
  • It worked and then stopped: the vault was locked. Unlock it in Custode.
