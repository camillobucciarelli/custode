The Health screen goes through your vault and lists the entries worth fixing: weak, reused or old passwords, duplicates, and entries that autofill cannot match.

All platforms5 minutes

Everything is checked on your device

The checks run on the vault you have already unlocked, without contacting anyone. Custode compares your passwords only with each other, inside your own vault.

Read the screen

Open Health: on a phone it is a tab in the bottom bar, on a computer it is in the sidebar. At the top there is a score from 0 to 100 with a short verdict: Looking great from 90, Mostly healthy from 70, Needs attention from 40, At risk below. Under it, each category shows how many entries it contains.

Weak passwords
Passwords that are too short or too predictable (the app measures them as under 40 bits of entropy).
Reused passwords
The same password used in two or more entries.
Old passwords
Passwords not changed in over two years. Entries with no recorded change date are not counted.
Duplicates
Entries for the same site and username. The number is the count of groups, not of entries.
Missing URL or username
Entries with neither a website nor a username, which autofill cannot match to a page.

Fix an entry

  1. Tap a category

    You get the list of the entries in it.

  2. Open an entry and choose Edit

    For a weak, reused or old password, set a new one: the button Generate secure password next to the password field creates it for you. For an entry in Missing URL or username, add the website or the username.

  3. Choose Save

    Change the password on the site as well: Custode updates only what is stored in the vault. Back on Health, the counts and the score are updated.

Merge duplicates

  1. Tap Duplicates

    Manage duplicates opens, with the entries in groups. In each group one entry is tagged Keep and the others Merge.

  2. Choose Merge and move duplicate

    What the merge does lists what will be copied into the entry you keep: notes, attachments, custom fields, websites, one-time code.

  3. Choose Merge and move duplicate again to confirm

    Or Cancel to leave everything as it is. Custode saves a dated copy of the vault, copies what is missing into the entry you keep and moves the other to the recycle bin.

Passwords are never merged. The entry you keep keeps its own password. Check that it is the right one before you confirm; the other entry stays in the recycle bin.
