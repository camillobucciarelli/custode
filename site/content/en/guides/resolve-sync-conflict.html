A conflict means the vault on this device and the file on Google Drive were both changed since the last sync. Custode never picks a side by itself: it stops and asks you.

All platforms5 minutes

Open the conflict

  1. Open Sync

    When there is a conflict the screen shows a card titled Both versions changed, with the line Tap to choose which version to keep.

  2. Tap the card

    You see the two versions, This device and Drive. When Drive reports it, the Drive one also shows the date it was last modified, which helps you tell which is the more recent.

The four choices

Keep local
Uploads the version on this device over the file on Drive. The version that was on Drive is not deleted: the app tells you it stays as a Drive revision.
Use remote
Downloads the file from Drive and puts it in place of the one on this device. Before replacing it, Custode saves a copy of your local file. If the copy cannot be made, nothing is replaced.
Review field by field
Opens a comparison of the two versions, so you can keep some things from one and some from the other. See the steps below.
Cancel
Changes nothing, on the device or on Drive. The conflict is shown again at the next sync.

Review field by field

  1. Choose Review field by field

    The Review merge screen opens. Nothing is written until you confirm at the end. Records and fields that exist on one side only are kept automatically: you are asked only about the things that differ.

  2. Go through the decisions

    They are grouped under Field conflicts, Deletions and Folders. Each one already has a suggested answer. Tap a decision to compare the two sides; Reveal values shows the hidden ones.

  3. Pick a side for each one

    Choose This device or Drive. For notes there is also Both, which keeps both notes one after the other. For a record deleted on one side only, choose Keep or Delete. Username, password and website move together: the side you pick is used for all three.

  4. Or answer everything at once

    Prefer local and Prefer remote pick the same side for every decision. When there are too many conflicts to review one by one, these two are the only options offered.

  5. Choose Continue, then Merge and sync

    The Ready to merge screen sums up what will happen. Edit decisions takes you back. With Merge and sync Custode saves a dated backup of your vault, writes the merged vault, uploads it to Drive and checks the upload. Do not close the app while it works.

  6. Choose Done

    Merge complete confirms that the merged vault is on this device and on Drive.

Where the copy is saved

The copy made by Use remote and by a merge is saved next to the vault file, with the same name followed by a number and .bak. On a computer you find it in the same folder as the vault. On a phone the vault lives in the app’s own storage, and the copy with it.

If the upload is not confirmed

After a merge you may see Upload not confirmed or Merge saved locally. The merged vault is already saved on this device, together with the backup of the previous version, but it is not marked as synced. Choose Check now to try again, or Close: the next sync checks what Drive holds and finishes safely.

If the vault or the Drive file changes while you review. Custode stops the merge and asks you to start the review again, so that no change is lost.
